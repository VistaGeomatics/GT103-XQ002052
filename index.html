Robotic Total Station

Topcon GT-1203

The GT-1203 is Topcon's world-class robotic total station engineered for single-operator survey and construction workflows. Powered by Ultra-Sonic Motors (USM) providing 180°/sec rotation, it delivers rapid target acquisition and tracking at up to 20°/sec — equivalent to 125 km/h at 100 metres. The instrument combines sub-second distance measurement, a 4.3" colour display, and MAGNET Field OnBoard software into a robust IP68-rated package.
